Bulgarian client · B2B trade platform · Live on Google Play

NaskoChina — trade, chat and pay in one app

Overview & architecture

A B2B platform connecting Bulgarian businesses with Chinese wholesale suppliers. Buyers browse verified listings, chat with sellers and agents, and pay from a multi-currency wallet (USD / EUR / RMB). I own the Flutter app (GetX, clean layered architecture) and work across its Node.js / Socket.IO backend.

Key engineering contributions

  • Real-time chat: moved messaging from Firestore listeners to a Socket.IO backend with a JWT-authenticated handshake — 1:1 and group chat, mentions, reactions, read receipts and settings sync.
  • AI assistant & chatbot: an in-app QRM assistant on the OpenAI Assistants API (threads and runs) that returns structured JSON the app routes by category, plus a GPT chat endpoint with image generation — JWT-protected on the Node.js backend.
  • Wallet & payments: QR send/receive, socket-driven payment requests, Stripe checkout and Google Pay top-ups.
  • Dynamic app links: deep-link routing that opens a profile, group, service or payment request straight from a shared URL or QR code.
  • Offline & media: ObjectBox local cache, background media uploads, and push notifications that route to the exact screen.
  • Backend: Express REST API + Socket.IO, Firebase Admin (FCM), Stripe, Twilio OTP, S3 media with ffmpeg video processing, Dockerised.

UK client · Food ordering & delivery · Freelance

Dessert Wala — dessert ordering with rewards

Overview & architecture

An ordering and delivery app for a UK dessert shop: customers browse the menu by category, pick up special offers, check out with Stripe and earn loyalty points on every order. Built in Flutter with GetX.

Key engineering contributions

  • Checkout: three-step Menu → Cart → Checkout flow with native Stripe payments and promo codes.
  • Loyalty points: £1 spent earns 1 point, with the points balance and its £ value shown across the app.
  • Delivery: Google Sign-In, map-based delivery address with Geolocator, and a rider flow for order hand-off.
  • Store hours: live open/closed banner in UK time, plus today's and past orders, favourites and push updates.
